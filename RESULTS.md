## 1. Phase 0 baseline bank

**Real Sentinel-2 (Punjab wheat belt), Wald-protocol 4× synthetic degradation.**
Frozen metric suite (`eval/metrics.py`). Reproduce: `python notebooks/phase0_baseline_bank.py`

| Method | PSNR (dB) ↑ | SSIM ↑ | SAM (°) ↓ | ERGAS ↓ | ΔNDVI ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bicubic | 31.47 | 0.882 | 1.00 | 3.00 | 0.0183 |
| lanczos | 31.34 | 0.877 | 1.02 | 3.05 | 0.0192 |
| esrgan  | 23.29 | 0.507 | 2.59 | 7.14 | 0.0338 |

**Reading this honestly:** bicubic and Lanczos already clear the pitch's SAM<5°/ΔNDVI≤0.05
gate with wide margin — that gate alone does not differentiate a real contribution from a
1980s interpolation kernel. ESRGAN's own NDVI error here (0.034) is smaller than the deck's
originally-cited 0.13; the deck number has been corrected to match this measurement. See
`FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md` section 0.3 for the full discussion of why this matters for the pitch.

## 2. opensr-test trustworthiness benchmark (real 5m ground truth)

**59 real Sentinel-2/VENµS scene pairs** (the same real-GT program that includes Kudaliar,
India). Metrics from `opensr-test` (ESA OpenSR): consistency (reflectance/spectral, vs the
LR input, computable with **no ground truth**), synthesis (genuine detail added), and
correctness (hallucination = fabricated detail, omission = missed detail, improvement =
correctly recovered detail). Reproduce: `python -m eval.opensr_bench --dataset venus`

| Method | Reflectance L1 ↓ | Spectral SAD ↓ | Synthesis ↑ | Hallucination ↓ | Omission ↓ | Improvement ↑ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bicubic | 0.0029 | 0.738 | 0.0128 | 0.140 | 0.294 | 0.566 |
| lanczos | 0.0029 | 0.714 | 0.0129 | 0.143 | 0.285 | 0.572 |
| esrgan  | 0.0144 | 4.365 | 0.0171 | **0.485** | 0.172 | 0.343 |
| **sen2sr** | 0.0067 | 1.749 | 0.0150 | 0.239 | 0.211 | 0.550 |

**The finding this table exists to make:** the SAM<5° gate alone cannot separate "real
detail" from "plausible-looking fabrication" — both bicubic and ESRGAN can post similar
spectral-consistency numbers on some scenes. Hallucination is the metric that actually
does: ESRGAN's hallucination (0.485) is >2× SEN2SR's (0.239) and >3× bicubic's (0.140),
which is the quantified version of the pitch's "generic AI upscaling distorts crop indices"
claim — now backed by a third-party benchmark rather than a single self-computed metric.

SEN2SR (ESA OpenSR, pretrained, CC0-1.0, `pip install sen2sr`) is a genuinely strong
existing baseline: more synthesis than bicubic, much less hallucination than ESRGAN. Any
claim of improvement over it needs to be measured, not assumed — see section 3.

## 3. SAM-loss ablation on real Indian ground truth (Kudaliar, Telangana)

**Real Sentinel-2/VENµS pairs, Kudaliar site** (SEN2VENµS dataset), 13 unique acquisition
dates, held out **by date** (no spatial leakage). 10m→5m real-GT ratio (×2), 32×32 LR crop
for fast CPU iteration. Reproduce: `python -m train.train_kudaliar --steps 200`

| Config | PSNR (dB) ↑ | SSIM ↑ | SAM (°) ↓ | ERGAS ↓ | ΔNDVI ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| λ_sam=0.0 (L1 only) | **23.80** | **0.556** | **7.04** | **143.3** | **0.118** |
| λ_sam=0.1 (L1+SAM+NDVI) | 21.72 | 0.530 | 8.41 | 162.8 | 0.131 |

**Honest result: on this run, the SAM/NDVI loss term did not help — it made every metric
slightly worse.** This is the opposite of the pitch's working thesis, and it is reported
here rather than hidden, per the plan's own instruction to report failure cases openly
(FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md's own risk register on this exact possibility). Do not put "SAM loss
proven to help" on a slide based on current evidence — it is not yet proven, on this data,
at this scale.

**Why this run is not the final word, stated plainly:**
- Only 200 steps, `embed_dim=60`, `depths=(2,2,2,2)`, CPU — a correctness/wiring check,
  not the Kaggle-scale training run the real answer should come from. Both configurations
  get equal, small step budgets; L1-only may simply converge faster in this short a window
  while the auxiliary spectral terms need more steps to pay off, or `λ_sam=0.1` may need
  re-tuning at this data/model scale (the plan's own sweep is `{0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0}` —
  only one value has been tested here).
- `batch_size>1` at Kudaliar's native 128×128 resolution triggers a Windows access
  violation in torch's batched matmul on this dev machine (confirmed via faulthandler,
  reproducible with synthetic data of the same shape too — an environment/threading issue,
  not a data or model-logic bug). Training here used a 32×32 center crop specifically to
  work around it at `batch_size=4`; this does not occur on Kaggle's GPU runtime, but it
  does mean this run trains on a smaller field-of-view than the native tile size.
- Kudaliar's real-GT ratio here is ×2 (10m→5m); the deployed product target is ×4
  (10m→2.5m) — see `FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md` section 1A.

**What this means for the plan:** the mandatory ablation (FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md section 2A) is
not yet closed. The next step is the real Kaggle-scale run — full resolution, more steps,
the full λ_sam sweep — before any spectral-loss claim goes on a pitch slide.

## 4. SRM (land-cover / mixed-pixel) head

The NTRO problem statement asks for "high-resolution land cover maps ... solving the
mixed pixel problem," which image super-resolution alone does not answer (see
`FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md` section 0.1). `models/srm_head.py` adds a second head, sharing the SR
model's encoder, that predicts per-class abundance **fractions** (softmax-normalized, sum
to 1 at every output pixel — the classical sub-pixel mapping solution) against real ESA
WorldCover 10m labels (`data/worldcover_fetch.py`).

**A real correctness bug was found and fixed here**: the first training script only
back-propagated the land-cover loss, so the SR head's weights never received gradient at
all (confirmed via a failed live API demo — the SR output was indistinguishable from
noise). `data/srm_dataset.py` now also returns the real, un-degraded chip as an SR
target, and `train/train_srm.py` trains both heads jointly (L1+SAM+NDVI for SR,
cross-entropy+sum-constraint for land cover). After this fix, trained on real Sentinel-2
across all three demo regions (768 chips, 400 steps, `python -m train.train_srm --steps
400`): SR loss 0.82 → 0.05, land-cover pixel accuracy → ~90% (still partly inflated by
Punjab's 90.6%-cropland class imbalance — a proper per-class IoU is still owed).

**This is the checkpoint the live pipeline now runs on, and it passes the release gate on
real data** — see section 7.

## 5. Ground-truth-free release gate

`infer/gate.py`: re-degrades the SR output back to the input's resolution (same
MTF-matched blur+decimate used in training) and checks spectral (SAM), radiometric (L1),
and NDVI consistency against the real measured input — computable on **any tile,
anywhere, with no ground truth**, which is what makes it deployable rather than just a
benchmark number. Unit-verified: passes a faithful upsample, fails a spectrally-scrambled
one (`python -m infer.gate`).

## 6. Geospatial pipeline

- `infer/tiler.py`: overlap + cosine-feather blending across tile boundaries. Verified
  against a single-shot pass (max abs diff 0.0002, no seam discontinuity).
- `infer/export_geotiff.py`: CRS-correct, transform rescaled to the SR pixel size, land-
  cover output includes a GDAL colour table for direct QGIS legend rendering.
- `infer/confidence.py`: per-pixel confidence via test-time-augmentation variance (no
  dropout layers exist to do MC Dropout without retraining, so this is the plan's own
  documented cheaper fallback) — flip/rotate the input, undo the transform on each
  output, take the per-pixel std-dev across runs.
- `infer/preview.py`: renders true-color and land-cover PNGs (percentile-stretched,
  downscaled to a browser-friendly size) so the demo shows real imagery, not just
  metric tables — GeoTIFFs need a tile server to view in-browser, out of scope here.
- `api/pipeline.py`: the fetch → tile → gate → (optional TTA) pipeline, shared by the
  live API (`api/main.py`) and the offline prebake script (`scripts/prebake_demo.py`) so
  the two paths can't silently diverge — this consolidation exists because an earlier
  version of `api/main.py` had a model-config mismatch (wrong `depths` vs the trained
  checkpoint) that only a live test caught.
- `scripts/prebake_demo.py`: runs the pipeline once per demo region and caches PNGs +
  GeoTIFFs + a gate report as static files under `web/demo_cache/` — the reliable path
  for an actual jury presentation (no live server, no live inference, no dependency on
  network/CPU/memory conditions during the demo itself). Each region runs in its own
  subprocess: this dev machine is memory-constrained enough that three regions back to
  back in one process OOM'd on the second/third even though each succeeds alone.

## 7. Live pipeline result — the gate on real data, both outcomes

After fixing the SR-head training bug (section 4), the full pipeline was run end to end
on real, live-fetched Sentinel-2 for all three demo regions:

| Region | Gate | SAM (°) | Reflectance L1 | ΔNDVI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Punjab wheat belt | **PASS** | 2.81 | 0.0201 | 0.0389 |
| Punjab wheat belt (2) | **PASS** | 3.03 | 0.0235 | 0.0395 |
| Kudaliar, Telangana | **FAIL** (bicubic served) | 6.25 | 0.0364 | 0.0852 |

Two honest findings here, both worth having in the pitch:

1. **The model generalizes to Punjab but not yet to Kudaliar** — training data was
   weighted toward Punjab-like scenes (see `train/train_srm.py`'s `TRAIN_WINDOWS`), and
   Kudaliar's different terrain/crop mix pushed SAM to 6.25°, just over the 5° threshold.
   This is exactly the generalization risk `FOUR_DAY_PLAN.md`'s own risk register named
   up front, now measured rather than assumed.
2. **The gate did its job.** It caught the out-of-distribution output and served bicubic
   instead of silently shipping a spectrally-wrong SR tile. This is the demo's strongest
   moment for the "trustworthy" pitch: show the jury a PASS (Punjab) and a FAIL
   (Kudaliar) side by side — the system knowing the difference is the actual product.

All outputs are correctly-georeferenced GeoTIFFs (verified: EPSG:32643, 2.5m pixel size,
correct band descriptions). Reproduce: `python -m scripts.prebake_demo` (writes
`web/demo_cache/`, open `web/index.html` via a static server to view).

## Honest summary for the pitch

1. The SAM<5°/ΔNDVI≤0.05 gate is real but not differentiating on its own — bicubic
   clears it easily too. Lead with hallucination/synthesis (section 2) for the
   trustworthiness story; use the gate result (section 7) to show the *system* working
   end to end on real data.
2. SEN2SR is a strong, free, pretrained competitor. The differentiators are the land-cover
   SRM output (section 4), real India validation (section 3), and the deployable
   ground-truth-free gate (section 5) — not "we built a better SR model from scratch."
3. The Kudaliar ablation (section 3) is preliminary; a converged Kaggle run is the
   remaining gap before this is a fully defensible number.
4. The live pipeline demo (section 7) is real and reproducible, and is the strongest
   "it actually works" evidence in this pack — lead the jury demo with it.
